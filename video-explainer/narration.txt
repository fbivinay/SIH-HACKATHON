# Kasauti — 2-minute explainer narration
# One segment per scene. Numbers are spelled out so Kokoro says them
# correctly; the screen shows the digits. Trimmed from a 401-word first pass
# that measured 2:45 — the real rate is ~145 wpm, not the 142 a single short
# line suggested.

[01 problem]
India publishes every rupee of M P LADS spending. Two lakh fifty thousand works. Nineteen thousand eight hundred crore rupees. Nobody has read them all.

[02 what]
Kasauti reads all of them, scores every work against comparable works, and hands officials a ranked list of what to verify.

[03 provenance]
The problem statement names the MoSPI portal. It publishes totals openly, but individual works only behind an O T P gated form. So we load the same record from Empowered Indian, and reconcile against MoSPI's own endpoints every night.

[04 score]
Each work scores zero to one hundred, from five components. Cost, twenty five percent. Delay, twenty five. Duplication, twenty. Agency, fifteen. Compliance, fifteen.

[05 forest]
Three models do what no rule could. An isolation forest learns what a normal sanction looks like for a sector and district, and finds the works sitting outside it.

[06 bert]
Sentence BERT embeds every description and compares them inside the same district, at ninety four percent similarity. That threshold was measured on this data, not borrowed.

[07 gemini]
Gemini Flash Lite labels the sectors keyword rules cannot read. It was chosen because it can abstain. It labels. It never scores, ranks, or flags.

[08 detectors]
Four more detectors describe agencies and members, not works. Year end payment bursts. First digit anomalies. Idle allocation. Uniform sanctions.

[09 queue]
Forty eight thousand works come out worth a second look. Two thousand three hundred are high risk. A morning's work, instead of a year's.

[10 why]
Open one, and it tells you why. Every reason carries the method behind it, so a flag can be argued with rather than obeyed.

[11 refresh]
It refreshes itself nightly. Load, label, score, reconcile.

[12 limits]
And it says what it cannot tell you. M P LADS publishes no progress percentage, no beneficiary count, no geo tag, so we never invent them. A kasauti says which pieces are worth assaying. Never which are false.
