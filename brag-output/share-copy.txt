LINKEDIN:

India publishes every MPLADS work — 2,50,839 of them across two Lok Sabha
terms, ₹19,804 crore. The record is open. It is also unreadable at that size.

Kasauti scores each work against comparable works in the same sector and
district using an isolation forest, Sentence-BERT for near-duplicate
detection, and Gemini to label the sectors that keyword rules cannot. 48,296
works come out worth a second look; 2,316 are high risk.

The part we care about most is the part that refuses: every flag carries the
method that produced it, so it can be argued with rather than obeyed, and the
site has a section saying what the published record does not support. A
kasauti says which pieces are worth assaying. Never which are false.
